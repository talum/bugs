# Ten Funny Poems

## 1. The Bug That Wouldn't Quit
A bug crept into my code at night,
I squashed it left, I squashed it right.
But every time I hit "compile,"
It came back with a bigger smile.

## 2. My Cat, the Critic
My cat sat down upon my keys,
And typed out `asdfjkl;` with ease.
The code review came back: "Looks great!"
Best pull request I've made to date.

## 3. The Sock Drawer Mystery
I put in ten socks, took out nine,
The dryer says that this is fine.
Somewhere a sock lives on the lam,
Perhaps in Bermuda, getting a tan.

## 4. Ode to Monday
Oh Monday, Monday, here you come,
You stomp on weekends just for fun.
My alarm clock laughs, my coffee's cold,
And somehow I've already been put on hold.

## 5. The Ambitious Snail
A snail set out to climb a tree,
"By Thursday lunch, the top I'll see!"
Six weeks later, still on the bark,
He'd made it up three inches — in the dark.

## 6. The WiFi Plea
Oh WiFi signal, strong and true,
Why must you vanish in the loo?
I only need one single bar —
You work fine in the neighbor's car.

## 7. Diet Starts Tomorrow
I'll eat a salad, skip the fries,
I'll exercise and early rise.
But then the pizza winked at me,
And well… the diet starts at three.

## 8. The Overzealous Autocorrect
I texted "I'll be there in five,"
My phone sent "I'll bee hair alive."
My friend replied, "Are you okay?"
Autocorrect ruins every day.

## 9. The Houseplant's Lament
They said I'm low-maintenance and tough,
That once-a-week watering was enough.
But here I sit, bone dry since June —
I'm basically a dusty prune.

## 10. The Meeting That Should've Been an Email
We gathered round the conference call,
Fifteen people, topics small.
An hour later, nothing done,
Could've been one sentence: "Looks good, hon."
